Caldiors: a premium baby-care brand website built with Next.js

By Kenil Vaghasiya · November 15, 2026 · 2 min read
  • Next.js
  • React
  • CSS3
  • Brand Design

Caldiors is a premium baby-care brand preparing to launch pant-style baby diapers in India. I designed and built its website with Next.js and React, from the first design through to launch. The site is live at caldiors.com.

The goal: earn a parent's trust before the product is on sale

Parents are careful buyers. A baby-care brand has to look trustworthy in the first few seconds, and Caldiors had an extra challenge: the product was not yet on sale. The website needed to introduce the brand, explain what is coming, and give interested parents a reason to leave their email.

The design: calm, soft and premium

The home page opens with a sleeping baby and one line: "Premium care for every little moment." The layout is mostly white space, with a classic serif logo, quiet typography and a single strong blue used only for actions. Nothing competes with the message.

The copy stays specific. The first screen says what the product is (premium pant-style baby diapers), where it is launching (India) and what it focuses on: absorption, softness and a comfortable fit.

A waitlist that gives something back

The main action on the page is an email field with a "Notify me" button. It comes with a first-customer offer: join the list and get 10% off the first Caldiors order. The same offer is repeated in the navigation, so it is always one click away. For a brand that has not launched yet, that list is the most valuable thing the site can produce.

More than a landing page

The site also has the pages a careful parent looks for:

  • Journal, for articles from the brand
  • About, for the story behind Caldiors
  • Size guide, so parents can choose the right fit
  • Contact

Why Next.js and React

I built Caldiors with Next.js, React and CSS3. For a brand site that will grow into a shop, that stack has clear advantages: pages load fast, search engines can read the content easily, and the same codebase can later take on products, checkout and customer accounts without starting again.

What I handled

  • Website design
  • Front-end and back-end development in Next.js and React
  • Responsive layouts for phone, tablet and desktop
  • Launch on the caldiors.com domain
