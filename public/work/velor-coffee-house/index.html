Velor Coffee House: coffee shop branding and a Framer website

By Kenil Vaghasiya · November 15, 2026 · 2 min read
  • Framer
  • Figma
  • Brand Design
  • Logo Design

Velor is a cosy neighbourhood coffee house. I created its full brand identity in Figma and designed and built its website in Framer. The aim was a look that feels friendly, earthy and easy to remember.

The brief: a coffee shop people remember

A neighbourhood coffee house competes with every other café on the same street. The brand has to do two jobs at once: look warm enough that people want to sit down, and be distinct enough that they recognise the cup in someone else's hand. That shaped every decision on Velor, from the logo to the last section of the website.

The logo: a coffee bean hidden in the "O"

The Velor logo is a soft, rounded retro wordmark. The detail that makes it stick is in the letter "O", which holds a coffee bean. It is small enough that the wordmark still reads cleanly on a sign across the road, and clear enough that it works as a mark on its own on a cup lid or a sticker.

The colour palette: sage, terracotta, cream and espresso

The palette has four colours:

  • Sage green, the calm base colour used on signage and apparel
  • Terracotta, the warm accent
  • Cream, for backgrounds and packaging
  • Deep espresso brown, for type and contrast

These are earthy colours that sit well next to wood, plants and coffee itself, so the brand looks at home inside the shop and not only on a screen.

The brand in the real world

A café brand is seen far more often on a cup than on a website, so the identity was designed across the things customers touch: storefront signage, staff apparel, takeaway cups and coffee bag packaging.

The website, built in Framer

The site opens on a single roasted coffee bean above a glass server, with the line "Good things. Take their time." and a short promise underneath: thoughtfully roasted coffee for the moments you want to stay in. A small "Scroll to savour" prompt invites visitors down the page, and the navigation stays out of the way.

I built it in Framer because it suits this kind of site well. The design goes from Figma to a published, responsive website without a long hand-off, the animation is part of the build and not an add-on, and the owner can change text and images later without calling a developer.

What was delivered

  • Logo and wordmark
  • Colour palette and typography
  • Takeaway cup and coffee bag packaging design
  • Staff apparel and storefront signage
  • A responsive website built in Framer
